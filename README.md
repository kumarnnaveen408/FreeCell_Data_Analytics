1. Customer Churn Analysis (Telecom)

Question: Which customer characteristics and services are linked to customers leaving?

Data: About 7,000 telecom customer records (tenure, contract type, services, billing, monthly charges, churn flag). Source: [add dataset name and link].

Approach

Cleaned and explored the raw data; built pivot tables (sheets Q1 to Q10) and charts to answer specific questions.
Compared churned and retained customers on tenure, monthly charges and service usage.

Key findings

Overall churn rate is about 26.5%.
Churned customers had a much shorter average tenure (about 18 months) than retained customers (about 38 months).
Churned customers paid more per month on average (about 74 vs about 61).
